    How Flibanserin Works in La Mesa

    Flibanserin acts on serotonin receptors in the brain, not on blood flow and not on hormones. The working theory is that it rebalances serotonin, dopamine and norepinephrine, the brain chemicals tied to sexual interest. It is not a hormone. Viagra works on blood flow, so the two are not comparable. Any change comes gradually, after several weeks of taking it every night.

    Who Flibanserin Is Meant For

    The FDA approval covers premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. In plain terms, desire used to be normal, it has dropped in most situations and with most partners, and that drop bothers you. Not every dip qualifies. Low desire driven mainly by depression, a medication such as an SSRI, a medical illness or a relationship problem is a different situation. Women past menopause fall outside the approved group.

    Dosing and the Nightly Schedule in La Mesa

    The dose is one 100 mg tablet, once a day, at bedtime. Always at bedtime. The side effects tend to hit within a few hours of the tablet, which is why the label puts the dose at bedtime and nowhere else. Unlike some options, it is not taken on demand. If you forget a night, skip it and take the next dose the following bedtime.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly about side effects, usually mild sleepiness or dizziness that eases. That part usually passes. Some women notice more interest by week four, and others need the full eight weeks before they can tell. In the clinical trials, the average gain over placebo was modest, about one additional satisfying sexual event a month, along with less distress. At eight weeks the label says to stop if there is no improvement, and your provider will ask you directly.

    Side Effects of Flibanserin in La Mesa

    The most common side effects are dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and fatigue. Dry mouth and trouble sleeping show up too. The serious risk is a sharp drop in blood pressure that can cause fainting, and it is more likely after alcohol or with drugs that raise flibanserin levels. Do not drive or run machinery for at least six hours after a dose. Message your provider the same day if you get lightheaded or unusually drowsy.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with your full medication list. Flibanserin cannot be combined with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, which include fluconazole, ketoconazole, clarithromycin, several HIV medicines and large amounts of grapefruit juice. Liver impairment of any degree rules it out. Expect questions about past fainting, blood pressure, depression, pregnancy plans and weekly drinks.

    Flibanserin Compared With PT-141 and Other Options in La Mesa

    Flibanserin and bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and often called PT-141, are the two FDA-approved medicines for HSDD before menopause. One is a pill every night and the other is a shot only when you want it. PT-141 has no daily alcohol timing, which matters to some women. Compounded options such as oxytocin are not FDA-approved for this use, and your provider will say so. Your provider explains which fits.
