    How Tadalafil Treatment Works in La Mesa, CA

    How Tadalafil Works in La Mesa

    Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor, the same drug class as sildenafil. With PDE5 blocked, cGMP builds up and relaxes the blood vessel walls in the penis, which lets blood flow in during arousal. Its half-life is about 17.5 hours. That is why one dose can last a day and a half. Arousal is still required.

    Who Tadalafil Is For

    Men with ED who want flexibility tend to like tadalafil. The as-needed dose suits men who want a longer window than sildenafil gives, such as a weekend away. Daily dosing makes sense for men who are intimate more than twice a week. Men with both ED and urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate can treat both with one daily 5 mg tablet, since tadalafil is FDA-approved for both conditions. Your provider makes the final call.

    Tadalafil Dosing Options in La Mesa

    As-needed dosing usually starts at 10 mg, taken 30 minutes to two hours before sex. It can go down to 5 mg or up to 20 mg. Daily tadalafil is 2.5 or 5 mg at about the same time each day, food or no food, reaching a steady level in roughly five days. One dose per day, either way. Unlike sildenafil, a heavy meal barely changes it.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    With as-needed tadalafil, most men notice a difference on the first or second try. Daily users usually feel it settle in by the end of week one. Headache, indigestion and back or muscle aches show up in some men during the first one to two weeks and often ease as the body adjusts to the medication. Keep a few notes. After about a month, your provider reviews how it is going.

    Tadalafil Side Effects in La Mesa

    Headache, indigestion, back pain and muscle aches are the most common side effects. The back pain tends to show up 12 to 24 hours after a dose and usually fades within two days. Flushing and a stuffy nose are also common. An erection lasting more than four hours, sudden vision loss or sudden hearing loss is an emergency and needs care right away, not a message. Rare, but real.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates in any form rule out tadalafil, including nitroglycerin and poppers, and so does riociguat. A recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain at rest and uncontrolled blood pressure all call for a closer look. Severe liver or kidney disease changes the dose. Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors like ketoconazole limit as-needed use to 10 mg once every 72 hours. Your provider also asks whether you can handle moderate exertion without chest pain.

    Tadalafil Compared to Sildenafil and Other Options in La Mesa

    Sildenafil works for four to five hours and is slowed by a heavy meal. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, meal or no meal. Compounded chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets often contain tadalafil too, but compounded versions are not FDA-approved, while generic tadalafil tablets are. Tadalafil causes more back aches. Your provider weighs those trade-offs with you.
