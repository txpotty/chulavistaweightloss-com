    How Tesamorelin Treatment Works in El Cajon, CA

    How Tesamorelin Works in El Cajon

    Tesamorelin is a stabilized version of growth hormone releasing hormone. It signals the pituitary to release growth hormone in pulses, which raises IGF-1 and pushes the body to break down fat, especially the visceral fat stored around the liver and intestines. Feedback controls stay active. Brand-name Egrifta SV is the approved product, and some pharmacies compound it.

    Who Tesamorelin Is Usually For

    The approved use is narrow, excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV who have lipodystrophy. Off-label, it may be considered for adults whose waist circumference and metabolic labs point to visceral fat as the main issue, sometimes with fatty liver. It is not a general weight loss drug. Pregnancy, active cancer and prior pituitary surgery or head radiation rule it out.

    Tesamorelin Dosing and Schedule in El Cajon

    Once a day, into the belly. Egrifta SV uses a fixed 1.4 milligram daily dose given under the skin of the abdomen, while compounded protocols can differ and your provider explains the exact amount. Rotate injection spots around the belly to cut down on redness. Pick the same time daily so it becomes routine.

    What to Expect Over Six Months

    The first month is mostly habit building. Clinical studies judged results at 26 weeks, when visceral fat had fallen by roughly 15 to 18 percent on average. Some people see the waistband loosen around months two and three. If there is little change at 26 weeks, the label and your provider both point toward stopping.

    Tesamorelin Side Effects in El Cajon

    Joint pain is common. Injection site redness, itching and pain, muscle aches and pain in the arms or legs are the other frequent ones. Some people get swelling in the hands or feet or numbness and tingling like carpal tunnel syndrome, and tesamorelin can raise blood sugar, which matters for anyone with prediabetes or diabetes. Report any rash or hives right away.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before prescribing, a provider reviews your history, medications and labs. Baseline testing usually includes A1C or fasting glucose, IGF-1 and a metabolic panel, and people with diabetes may be asked about recent eye exams because of retinopathy risk. You will be asked about cancer, pituitary conditions and pregnancy plans. Lab reports can be uploaded as photos.

    Tesamorelin Compared to Related Options in El Cajon

    Sermorelin is the closest relative. Both are growth hormone releasing hormone analogs, but tesamorelin is more stable in the body and has trial data for visceral fat while sermorelin does not. The CJC-1295 and ipamorelin pairing works on a similar axis without any FDA approval. Semaglutide and tirzepatide reduce appetite and total weight, so they solve a different problem.
