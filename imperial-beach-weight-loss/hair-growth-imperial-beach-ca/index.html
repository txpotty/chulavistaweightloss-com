    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Imperial Beach, CA

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Imperial Beach

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. The two main medicines are finasteride, which cuts DHT, and minoxidil, which lengthens the growth phase. Used together, they usually beat either one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Pattern loss is the usual reason, though low ferritin, thyroid trouble and postpartum shedding can mimic it. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Off-label oral options, such as spironolactone, are added when appropriate.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Imperial Beach

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. Some men use a compounded topical spray that combines both, which is not FDA-approved but may reduce whole-body exposure to finasteride.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. Approved for prostate enlargement, it is used off-label for hair, usually after finasteride falls short. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil is added for growth.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Imperial Beach

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. The blend has no FDA approval. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in Imperial Beach

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. That is why most treatments take three to six months to show a visible change and around twelve months to reach their full effect, even when they are working from the first week. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Results last only as long as treatment does.
